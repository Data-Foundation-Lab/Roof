# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behaviour and Scope

Roof welcomes participation regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual orientation, experience or other personal characteristics. Treat people with respect, offer constructive feedback, consider the effects of your actions, and respect others' boundaries and privacy.

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and other DFLab-managed project spaces, and to conduct when officially representing Roof elsewhere. Technical disagreement is welcome when expressed respectfully.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing private information without permission, and retaliation against reporters or witnesses are unacceptable. Repeated disruption or deliberate evasion of moderation is also unacceptable.

## Private Reporting

Report concerns to [roof-security@dflb.org](mailto:roof-security@dflb.org), the interim private conduct-reporting contact monitored by [Steve Yoo (@dflbsteve)](https://github.com/dflbsteve) (DFLab Maintainer). Use a subject such as `Private Conduct Report: Roof`. This mailbox also handles security reports; label the subject to help route your report.

Describe what happened, when and where, who was involved, any immediate concerns, and your preferred contact method. Share only necessary evidence and omit unrelated personal information. You may report conduct affecting someone else. Do not publish a report as a public issue.

Access is limited to people necessary to assess and resolve the report. They must protect identities and evidence, share the minimum necessary information, and explain material limits to confidentiality where possible. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised when investigation, immediate safety or applicable obligations require limited disclosure.

## Reporting and Enforcement Responsibilities

Steve is accountable for receiving reports, acknowledging them, assessing immediate needs, collecting relevant accounts fairly, deciding proportionate action, communicating decisions privately and keeping a restricted record. The acknowledgement target is three business days; it is not a guarantee of continuous monitoring. Reporters should follow up through the same private channel if no acknowledgement arrives.

Steve must recuse himself from decisions about complaints involving him or another conflict of interest. DFLab must designate an uninvolved reviewer before investigating or deciding those complaints, or reviewing an appeal against Steve's decision. Reports to this mailbox may be seen by Steve before reassignment. A reporter may initially request an independent contact without sending sensitive details; DFLab is responsible for supplying that contact privately.

## Enforcement

The responsible, unconflicted reviewer considers severity, impact, context, recurrence and relevant evidence. Where appropriate, the person reported should have an opportunity to respond. Immediate temporary restrictions may be used to protect participants while a report is assessed.

Responses may include a request to stop or correct behaviour, removal of content, a private warning, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban for severe or repeated misconduct. Serious misconduct may warrant a ban without prior warnings. Explain the applicable restrictions, duration and reasons privately, while protecting the reporter and other participants. Enforce decisions consistently and prohibit retaliation.

A person affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the same private contact, providing relevant new information or explaining a procedural concern. An appeal must be assessed by an uninvolved DFLab reviewer, not solely by the person whose decision is challenged. Appeals remain open until that independent assessment is complete.

Keep reports, evidence and enforcement records access-restricted; periodically review access and retain only what is necessary for enforcement, accountability and applicable obligations. Do not publish identifying details without an appropriate basis. Steve maintains this policy and DFLab is responsible for appointing independent coverage and replacing the interim arrangement when feasible.
